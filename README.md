# Verdant Plains

A 3D fantasy RPG that runs in your browser. Explore Greenhaven and the plains around it, take quests from the Adventurer's Guild, and fight slimes, wolves, goblins and the King of the Wolves as a Warrior, Ranger or Mage.

The whole game is in `index.html`. Turn on GitHub Pages (Settings, Pages, branch `main`) to play it from a link.

## Controls

- Click to capture the mouse and look around (Esc frees it)
- WASD or arrow keys: walk (hold Shift to sprint)
- Left click: attack
- Space: jump
- Q: class skill
- R: drink a potion
- E: talk to buildings, open chests
- I: bag, M: world map
